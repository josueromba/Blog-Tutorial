# Brief - jobs/demo-001

**Label:** "studio demo". The owner gave no inputs, so this is an original ad for the studio itself.

## Audience
SaaS founders and marketing leads, mostly B2B and early-stage, who need a short launch or product video and scroll LinkedIn/X on a phone.

## Message
"Your SaaS launch video in 15 seconds, made in code": motion design built with Remotion, delivered as 16:9 + 9:16 with editable source.

## Required text (exact wording, English)
1. Hook (0-1 s): "Your launch deserves motion."
2. Offer: "15 s SaaS video"
3. Formats: "16:9 + 9:16"
4. Source: "Editable Remotion source"
5. Revision: "1 revision included"
6. End card: "Claude Code Motion Design Studio" + small tag "studio demo"

No prices, no client logos, no claims about customers or results, and no contact details (the owner hasn't approved any).

## Assets
- All local and generated by code: shapes, a mock SaaS UI (fictional, unbranded), gradients, type.
- Fonts: a locally bundled font (e.g. @remotion/google-fonts loaded at bundle time is fine only if it renders offline once installed; otherwise a system font stack). State which.
- Sound: original, synthesised programmatically by a script in the repo that writes a WAV to `public/studio/demo-001/` (deterministic, no samples downloaded). Hits land on the scene cuts.
- If you make anything that counts as an asset, record its origin in `jobs/demo-001/assets/SOURCES.md`.

## Formats and render
- `Demo001Landscape`: 1920x1080. `Demo001Vertical`: 1080x1920, recomposed for vertical rather than cropped.
- 30 fps, 450 frames (15.0 s), H.264 yuv420p + AAC.
- Render: `npx remotion render src/studio/index.ts <CompId> jobs/demo-001/out/<file>.mp4 --codec=h264 --pixel-format=yuv420p --audio-codec=aac`
- Outputs: `jobs/demo-001/out/demo-001-16x9.mp4`, `jobs/demo-001/out/demo-001-9x16.mp4`

## Acceptance criteria
- A hook is visible and moving by frame 30.
- Text is readable on a phone: in 9:16, body text is at least ~56 px and headlines at least ~96 px, everything sits inside 8% safe margins, and each line stays on screen at least 1.2 s at full opacity.
- The sound has a clear hit on every cut; the audio stream runs the full length.
- Deterministic, with local assets only. No clipping, overlap or off-frame text in either format.
- Brand consistent: one palette, one type family, the same end card in both formats.
- Both files pass the ffprobe check (size, 30/1, 450 counted frames, h264, yuv420p, AAC stream, duration 15.0 s ±1 frame, decodes cleanly).

## Paths
Source in `src/studio/` (entry `src/studio/index.ts`). Storyboard in `jobs/demo-001/storyboard.md`. QA in `jobs/demo-001/qa/`. Leave `src/remotion-rssi/` untouched.
