# Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion: verified facts

Research date: 2026-10-09. Only public pages were used. Nothing was logged in or paywalled.

## Summary

- **Claude Dashboards**: exists as an official Anthropic/Claude feature. Official name is "Claude Dashboards" (plural). Beta on paid plans. Announced 2026-10-08.
- **Claude Motion**: exists as an official Anthropic/Claude feature. Official name is "Claude Motion". Beta on Team and Enterprise. Announced 2026-10-08.
- Both are announced in one official post: "Build live dashboards and animate explainers with Claude".
- Not found on anthropic.com/news (checked 2026-10-09). Not found on code.claude.com/docs "What's new" (latest entry Week 37, Sept 7–11, 2026).
- Not to be confused with the older "Live artifacts in Claude Cowork" (support article 14729249, dashboards in the desktop app, April 2026). That is a different feature.

## Claude Dashboards

- **Exists as official feature?** Yes. Official name: "Claude Dashboards".
- **Announcement date:** 2026-10-08 (page reads "October 8, 2026").
- **URL:** https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion (also listed on https://claude.com/blog as "Build live dashboards and animate explainers with Claude", Oct 8).
- **What it does (quoted or close paraphrase):**
  - "Claude can turn your data into a dashboard that stays current." (https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion)
  - Connects to "a data platform like BigQuery, Databricks or Snowflake, or a CRM tool like Salesforce." (same URL)
  - "Clicking a number shows its underlying query. Each chart shows when its data was last refreshed." (same URL)
  - Dashboards can be sent to Amplitude, Grafana, Hex, Mixpanel, Omni, Perplexity, PostHog and Sigma. Looker, monday.com and Tableau are "coming soon." (same URL)
  - "Dashboards work well for quick, exploratory questions." (same URL)
- **Audience and availability:** Beta, "in beta on paid plans" (same URL). Admin setting: off by default for Enterprise; admins enable it under Organization settings > Artifacts (same URL).
- **Tagline or key phrasing:** "a dashboard that stays current" (same URL). No separate tagline found.
- **Brand or press guidelines:** Anthropic Trademark Guidelines, https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines (effective date August 1, 2024, per the page). Key rules quoted: "You may only use our trademarks as specifically permitted by us and only in materials we approve beforehand." "No alterations of our trademarks (changes to color, font, proportion, or otherwise) are permitted." "Do not use a trademark symbol with the trademarks." Note: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/brand-guidelines returned 404 (checked 2026-10-09).

## Claude Motion

- **Exists as official feature?** Yes. Official name: "Claude Motion".
- **Announcement date:** 2026-10-08 (same post as above).
- **URL:** https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion
- **What it does (quoted or close paraphrase):**
  - "Builds a short animated explainer from a prompt." Start it by "Typing "/motion" in the Claude message box" (same URL).
  - "You can adjust it in the editor or ask Claude to change it, then download it as an MP4 file." (same URL)
  - "Animations are code that controls text, charts, shapes, and images." (same URL)
  - "It doesn't use a video generation model, so there's no generated footage and no AI-generated people." (same URL)
  - Open projects in Adobe, Descript, HeyGen, Higgsfield, invideo, Luma AI and Runway. Canva and Captions are "coming soon." (same URL)
- **Audience and availability:** Beta, "Claude Motion is in beta on Team and Enterprise." Built for "explaining your own work." Admin setting: off by default for Enterprise; enabled under Organization settings > Artifacts (same URL).
- **Tagline or key phrasing:** "Claude Motion is built for explaining your own work." No separate tagline found.
- **Brand or press guidelines:** Same as Claude Dashboards, see the Anthropic Trademark Guidelines above.
